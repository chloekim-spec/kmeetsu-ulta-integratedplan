What We Are Asking Ulta For

Goodai Global USA runs all 24 initiatives and funds 9 of them outright. What we cannot build alone is the Ulta half — the shelf, the page, the flyer and the handle. Fifteen asks, split by what each actually costs Ulta, each with the date it has to be decided.

3 decisions due by October 2026 February 2027 is not a February decision. Endcap, mailer space and kit SKU set-up all close 4–6 months ahead — miss them and the first moment moves to August.

Lead times are planning assumptions based on standard retail cycles and require confirmation from the relevant Ulta teams. Dates shown unlock Moment 01 (February 2027); the August cycle repeats the same sequence six months later.
