## 1. Why these images exist

Goodai Global USA is proposing an integrated annual retail program to **Ulta Beauty leadership**, branded *K-Beauty Week presented by KMEETSU*. The proposal runs as an interactive HTML timeline covering October 2026 through December 2027.

The timeline shows four national moments a year, each firing 19 marketing initiatives simultaneously, plus an always-on baseline in between. Every initiative on that timeline is a dot. **Hovering a dot opens a small preview card, and these 18 images are what fills those cards.**

That makes the job specific. The images are not decoration and they are not a moodboard. Ulta's leadership has so far understood this program as a list of separate line items — a promotion here, a shelf there, some media. The purpose of the images is to make one thing undeniable in a single glance: **all of these things happen at the same time, in the same store, on the same week.**

Two ideas have to survive the thumbnail:

1. **Scale.** This is a multi-brand program across hundreds of doors with a national field team, not a single-brand activation in eleven stores.
2. **Consequence.** The end state is shoppers physically queuing inside Ulta for K-Beauty. If someone remembers one frame from this deck, it should be that one (IMG-10).

**Critical constraint:** each image renders at **300 × 158px** inside the hover card. Open your file at that size before you submit it. If the subject isn't instantly readable — a person, a product, a crowd, a shelf — it fails, regardless of how good it looks at full size. **Small text, fine print and busy backgrounds all disappear.** When in doubt, crop tighter.

## 4. How to source each one

Match the method to the asset. Using the wrong method is the most common failure here.

**Design mockup — not photography.** IMG-03, 05, 07, 08, 14, 16, 17.
Ulta is looking at how *its own* assets get used. A real photo of someone else's flyer is worse than a clean mockup of ours. Build these in Figma or equivalent using the KMEETSU brand palette (magenta `#E8186D`, orange `#F5741D`, deep plum `#2D1B2E`, cream `#FDF8F3`) and the current Ulta site/app layout as reference.

**Product photography or 3D render.** IMG-04, 06, 11.
These are real physical items or fixtures. If the Discovery Kit and sample kit don't exist yet, a render or a styled flat-lay of the actual component products is acceptable — but the multi-brand mix has to be genuine, using our real brands.

**In-store photography — real or composed.** IMG-01, 02, 09, 10, 12, 18.
Preferred: our own field team photos from actual Ulta doors. If we don't have them, compose or generate them, but keep the store environment plausibly Ulta-like without reproducing Ulta trade dress inaccurately. **IMG-10 will almost certainly need to be created rather than found** — the queue is the whole point and we don't have that photo yet.

**Existing assets, resize only.** IMG-12, 13, 15.
Already in the project image library: `slide-fieldteam.jpg` (→ IMG-12), `slide-roundrobin.jpg` (→ IMG-13), `slide-social.jpg` (→ IMG-15), `slide-babox-revise.jpg` (→ IMG-13 alternate), `Beauty_brand_ambassador_group_portrait….png` (→ IMG-15 alternate). ## 6. Hard rules — do not include

- **Competing retailers.** No Olive Young, Target, Sephora, Amazon or similar store interiors, logos or packaging. This document goes to Ulta.
- **Real numbers.** No actual sales figures, dollar amounts, unit volumes or margin data anywhere, including inside chart mockups. IMG-17 uses dummy figures.
- **Identifiable Ulta employees.** No recognisable faces of Ulta staff.
- **Modified Ulta branding.** Never recolour, stretch, crop or restyle the Ulta logo. Use the approved asset as-is.
- **Unlicensed imagery.** No screenshots of other brands' campaigns, no unlicensed stock, no lifted press photos. Everything must be ours, licensed, or clearly a mockup.
- **Faces of minors.** Back To School is a moment in this program, but no under-18 talent in any frame.

## 8. Glossary

Retail terms used above, in case any are unfamiliar:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
**Tentpole** | A retailer's own major sales event on its annual calendar (Black Friday, Back To School, 21 Days of Beauty). Brands plan around these.
**Endcap** | The display bay at the end of a store aisle. Prime real estate; allocated by the retailer's merchandising team.
**In-line** | The regular shelf position a brand occupies within its category, as opposed to a temporary display.
**Secondary placement** | Any incremental display outside a brand's normal shelf — promo table, etagere, floor stand.
**Etagere** | A freestanding multi-tier display fixture. Ulta's version is typically 5 tiers with front and back facings.
**POG (planogram)** | The shelf layout diagram that dictates which product sits where. Changed only at scheduled category resets.
**GWP** | Gift With Purchase — a free item unlocked by a qualifying purchase.
**Circular / mailer** | The retailer's printed promotional flyer, mailed or in-store. Front page placement is the most valuable and closes months ahead.
**Co-post** | An Instagram post published jointly by two accounts, appearing in both feeds. Materially stronger than a repost.
**Sell-through** | Percentage of delivered inventory actually sold in a period. The core measure of whether an activation worked.
**New-to-brand** | Share of purchasers buying the brand for the first time. The metric that matters most for a category-building program.
**Sampling / trial** | Getting product into a shopper's hands free, to convert them later. The primary mechanic of the light moments.
